# Execution Log

**Fill this in as you execute. Do not pre-populate it.**

An execution log with results you did not observe is the single most damaging thing you
could put in this repository — an interviewer will pick a row and ask what happened.
A partially completed log with honest gaps is worth far more than a complete invented one.
